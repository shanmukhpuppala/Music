About

Scientist by education. Musician by purpose.

I hold a Master's degree in Organic Chemistry and worked as a Junior Scientist at Laurus Labs, where I built a strong foundation in research, analysis and problem-solving.

But music has always been close to me. My passion for singing and learning music eventually became my profession — today I am a full-time music educator with eight years of teaching experience across Carnatic vocals, western vocals and guitar.

As an educator, I aim to make learning structured, practical and enjoyable — whether a student wants to sing, learn guitar, build confidence, or simply discover a deeper connection with music.
